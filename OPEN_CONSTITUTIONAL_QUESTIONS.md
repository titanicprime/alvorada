# Open Constitutional Questions

## Status

Every item below is `UNRESOLVED`. This register does not answer, authorize, or narrow any
question.

1. What rules govern Constitutional Authority succession?
2. Which human powers are exactly non-delegable?
3. How should the Threshold of Reliance be calibrated by consequence and domain?
4. What emergency powers may exist, and what scope, duration, review, expiry, and restoration
   controls must bind them?
5. What weight, if any, should precedent carry?
6. When and how may emergent practice be formalized without laundering effective power into
   authority?
7. What thresholds apply to constitutional amendment, structural amendment, and refounding?
8. Where are the exact boundaries of artificial autonomy over means?
9. At what effective-power thresholds is human intervention mandatory?
10. What is the final wording of Constitution version 2.0?
